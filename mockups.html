Front view: assembled on the wall

250 mm wide (P2S) · 40 mm header · 24 mm per result tile · the seams are 0.4 mm chamfers

Side section: sliding dovetail joint

This cut goes through the seam between two tiles (the wall is on the left). The rail on the lower tile is held inside the channel of the tile above. Gravity and forward pull are both carried by the dovetail flanks.

Assembly: slide in from the side

The channel is open on the left and closed on the right (end stop). A 0.4 mm detent clicks the tile into place. The optional pin hole gives a permanent lock.

Back view: header tile

Two blind keyhole slots 200 mm apart for #8 / M4 screws. The channel runs along the bottom edge.

Keyhole detail

Ø9 entry, a 4.5 mm slot that runs 8 mm upward, and a 2.5 mm ledge that captures the screw head.
